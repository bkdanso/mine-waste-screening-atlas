How to read the scores

Critical Mineral Index (CMI): how far nearby samples exceed typical U.S. soil for elements on the 2025 U.S. critical minerals list; sum of the five largest log10 enrichment factors, each capped at 1000x (range 0 to 15). Heavy-metal Hazard Index (HHI): sum of each metal's median divided by its EPA residential soil screening level (November 2024 table); 1 means one screening level reached. Priority: Dual = top third on both; Resource or Hazard = top third on one. Confidence: A = 5+ samples within 1 km including one taken on mine waste; B = 3+ within 1 km; C = fewer, or only 1 to 2 km away; U = none within 2 km. Exceeding a screening level means "look closer", not "unsafe". Samples near a pile may be ore or host rock rather than the waste itself.

Sources: U.S. Geological Survey (2026) National Mine Waste Inventory v2.0, doi:10.5066/P148EEUA; Granitto and others (2026) geochemical data 1962 to 2023, doi:10.5066/P1FXE9GK; U.S. EPA Regional Screening Levels (Nov 2024); Final 2025 List of Critical Minerals (90 FR, Nov 7, 2025).
